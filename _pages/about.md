I am the Chief Data Scientist at [Medius](https://medius.si), a Slovenian-based
company specializing in custom software development. I hold a Bachelor's degree
in Electrical Engineering from the University of Ljubljana, which I completed
in 2009. Subsequently, I pursued a Master's degree with a focus on Automation
and Intelligent Systems, where I developed a keen interest in artificial
intelligence, particularly machine learning and data mining.

My academic journey continued with a PhD program at the University of
Ljubljana, where my research centered on pattern recognition, specifically in
speech-related technologies. My PhD research involved emotional recognition
from speech and the development of cross-lingual parametric speech synthesis
systems. My academic contributions can be accessed on
[ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Tadej-Justin) and [Google
Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=405SSpEAAAAJ).

In parallel with my studies, I co-founded a micro-enterprise focused on
consulting and developing web-based applications using Python. Our projects
leveraged solutions like Plone CMS and frameworks such as
[Pyramid](https://trypyramid.com/) or
[Flask](https://flask.palletsprojects.com/). My expertise in Automation
fostered a deep interest in DevOps practices, including Continuous Deployment
and Continuous Integration. With the advent of Kubernetes, I closely followed
its evolution and earned my [Kubernetes
Administrator](https://www.youracclaim.com/badges/5a5265c2-a855-4524-8195-94a287da6725)
certification in late 2019.
